## Why this works (and what to expect)

A page stuck at 8-12 with good on-page signals is almost always being held down by an **authority gap** vs. the incumbents (OPM, FedWeek, FedSmith, federalpensionadvisors, MyFederalRetirement). Closing that gap means earning links from relevant, trusted domains. Realistic timeline: the first earned links land in 2-4 weeks; ranking movement typically follows 4-12 weeks after links are indexed. This is a compounding program, not a one-shot.

**Two link types, both useful, different jobs:**
- **Followed editorial links** (earned PR, guest columns, association resource pages, .gov/.edu) → move rankings. Prioritize these.
- **Sponsored/nofollow links** (KSL BrandView, most paid placements) → referral traffic, brand lift, local trust, and a real audience of federal employees. They rarely pass ranking equity directly, but they drive conversions and seed brand searches (which *are* a ranking signal). Don't overpay for them as if they were followed links.
