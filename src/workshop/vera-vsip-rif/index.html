The vocabularyWebinar · 02
The vocabulary of a downsizing

Three offers, three very different things.

VERA
Voluntary Early Retirement

An agency-authorized chance to retire earlier than normal eligibility — and collect your pension now, with no age reduction.

VSIP
Voluntary Separation Incentive

A lump-sum buyout — cash to leave voluntarily. Often paired with VERA during a downsizing.

RIF
Reduction in Force

An involuntary separation when positions are cut. May come with severance pay if you are not yet retirement-eligible.

One more you may hear

DRP — Deferred Resignation Program. A separate "resign now, stay on the rolls for a period" offer. Same rule: read exactly what it does to your pay status, benefits, and retirement eligibility before you sign.

Part I · VERAVERA · 01 / 03
Two paths to early eligibility

Age 50 with 20 — or any age with 25.

Path 1
50 / 20
Age 50 + 20 years of service
Path 2
25
Any age + 25 years of service
It is the agency's offer, not yours
  • Your agency must be authorized by OPM to offer VERA — usually during a reorg, downsizing, or RIF
  • It is offered in a defined window to a defined group — not a benefit you can request on your own
  • You still need the service time above on the agency's chosen date
Why it matters

VERA is the only common way to start an unreduced FERS pension well before your Minimum Retirement Age — which is what makes an early-out worth a serious look.

VERA · Eligibility07
Part I · VERAVERA · 02 / 03
What VERA does — and does not — change

No age reduction. One timing catch.

Pension
Computed normally.

Your FERS annuity uses the standard formula — years × 1.0% × High-3. There is no early-retirement reduction like MRA+10.

Supplement
Waits until your MRA.

The FERS Supplement is payable — but generally not until you reach your Minimum Retirement Age, not your VERA date. Plan for that gap.

Insurance
FEHB & FEGLI for life.

If you were enrolled for the 5 years before retiring, you can generally carry FEHB and FEGLI into retirement — a major reason VERA is valuable.

For most people, lifetime health coverage is worth as much as the pension itself. Confirm your 5-year enrollment before you commit.
VERA · Pension & benefits08
Part I · VERAVERA · 03 / 03
An honest look

A great supplement — not always a full paycheck.

Retiring at 50 or 52 means a smaller pension than retiring at 60 — fewer years in the formula, and the Supplement not yet flowing. For many, the math works because they keep working in a second career while the pension and health coverage run underneath.

What you keep — for life
  • A pension that pays for life, with annual cost-of-living adjustments
  • Federal health insurance you can carry into retirement
  • The freedom to take another job without losing the pension
What to plan around
  • A smaller pension than waiting would produce
  • A gap before the FERS Supplement begins at your MRA
  • Replacing income until Social Security and the Supplement arrive

Part II · VSIPVSIP · 01 / 02
How much, and the strings attached

Up to $25,000 — or $40,000 at Defense.

Most agencies
$25K
Typical statutory maximum payment
Dept. of Defense
$40K
Higher cap authorized for DOD
The strings
  • Paid as a lump sum — and taxed as ordinary income
  • If you return to federal service within 5 years, you generally must repay it in full
  • It does not increase your High-3 or your pension
  • Offered in a limited window, like VERA
Note

Your agency may offer less than the maximum — the cap is a ceiling, not a promise.

VSIP · The basics11
Part II · VSIPVSIP · 02 / 02
The part the packet skips

$25,000 is not $25,000 in your pocket.

01
The tax bite is real.

A lump sum lands in one tax year and can push you into a higher bracket. What you keep may be noticeably less than the headline number.

02
It can stack with VERA.

When an agency offers both, you may take VERA and VSIP together — the early pension plus the cash. Often the strongest version of an early-out.

03
It is not severance.

VSIP is a voluntary incentive. Severance pay is a separate involuntary benefit — and you generally cannot collect both for the same separation.

The question to ask

"After taxes, is the buyout worth more to me than what I would receive another way?" Run the after-tax number, not the headline number.

Part III · RIF & SeveranceRIF · 01 / 05
How the order is decided

A RIF follows rules, not just headcount.

When positions are eliminated, who stays is set by a retention order — not by a manager's preference. Four factors decide your place on the list.

01
Tenure

Permanent/career employees rank above term and probationary.

02
Veterans' Preference

Eligible veterans receive added retention standing.

03
Length of Service

Creditable service — including credited military time — improves standing.

04
Performance

Recent performance ratings can add service credit toward retention.

Know your rights

A RIF usually comes with a written notice (often 60 days) and may grant "bump and retreat" rights to move into a position held by someone with lower standing. Read the notice carefully — the clock starts when it arrives.

  • Concern: ratings can be subjective and vary by office and supervisor

How severance pay is built

Weeks of pay — boosted after age 40.

Basic severance allowance
  • 1 week of pay for each of the first 10 years
  • 2 weeks of pay for each year beyond 10
  • Partial years add 25% per full quarter
Weekly payhow it's set = Annual salarybasic pay ÷ 52weeks/year
Age adjustment & cap
  • Over age 40: add 2.5% for each full quarter beyond 40
  • At age 45 that is roughly a +50% boost; at 55, about +150%
  • Lifetime maximum: 52 weeks of pay total

One more path most packets don't name

DSR — when the agency pushes you out.

Discontinued Service Retirement — sometimes called involuntary retirement — is a special FERS retirement that opens up when you're separated against your will and meet the age & service bar. There is no age reduction.

FERS eligibility
  • Age 50 with at least 20 years of creditable service, or
  • Any age with at least 25 years of creditable service
  • And the separation must be involuntary — not for cause
What can trigger DSR
  • A RIF that abolishes your position
  • A transfer of function or reorganization
  • A directed reassignment you cannot accept
  • A position abolishment outside a formal RIF
DSR is the bridge for someone who is not yet retirement-eligible by the normal rules but is being pushed out anyway. It can turn what looked like a severance situation into a lifetime pension situation.
Decision · DSR overview21
Part IV · Your DecisionDecision · 03 / 06
How DSR and RIF interact

A RIF can open DSR — but not every RIF does.

01
RIF may create eligibility.

If a RIF abolishes your position and you meet the 50/20 or any-age/25 bar, you may qualify for DSR — and start an unreduced pension instead of taking severance.

02
Not every RIF leads to DSR.

You may be reassigned, you may not meet the service bar, you may decline a reasonable offer, or you may resign voluntarily. DSR is not automatic.

03
DSR without a RIF.

A formal RIF notice is not required. A transfer of function, reorganization, or directed reassignment can also open DSR.

The "reasonable offer" rule

If the agency offers a position in your commuting area, with similar tenure and similar grade or pay, and you decline it — DSR eligibility can be lost. The definition is fact-specific; read the offer carefully.

FERS Supplement under DSR

DSR retirees can receive the FERS Supplement before age 62 — but, like VERA, not until your MRA. Plan for the same income-bridge gap.

What we see go wrong

Four avoidable mistakes.

01
Assuming a RIF is coming

Turning down a VSIP because you expect severance — even though a RIF is never guaranteed. Compare the offer in hand to the realistic alternatives, not the imagined ones.

02
Missing the 5-year rule

Walking away without confirming FEHB and FEGLI eligibility — and losing lifetime coverage that is hard to replace.

03
Forgetting the gap

Not planning for the income gap before the FERS Supplement and Social Security begin.

04
Beating the deadline

Signing under pressure on the last day instead of running the numbers as soon as the offer appears.
