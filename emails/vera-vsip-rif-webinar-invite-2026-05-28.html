Agencies are still weighing early-retirement and buyout offers — and many federal employees are being asked to make a decision on a short clock. If your agency is talking about a VERA, a VSIP, or a Reduction in Force, the time to understand your numbers is before an offer arrives, not during the few days you get to accept or decline it.

What we’ll cover

1

What VERA, VSIP, and a RIF actually mean.

How each one can change your pension, your FERS Supplement, any severance, and — critically — your federal health insurance.

2

Whether you may be eligible to go early.

VERA can let you retire at age 50 with 20 years of service, or at any age with 25 years — often without the usual early-retirement reduction, while keeping your pension and your health insurance.

3

How to run your numbers ahead of time.

So if an offer comes your way, you can weigh it calmly — in days, not in a panic — and know whether to take it, wait, or plan a different exit.
