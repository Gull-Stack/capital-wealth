# Forbidden phrases and compliant rewrites

Context matters — a banned word inside an educational *definition* ("a fee-only advisor is one who…") can be acceptable; the same word *claimed about Capital Wealth* is a violation. Always read the sentence, not just the token.

## About the firm — never say → say instead
| Never say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| fee-only / fee-only fiduciary | False; CW earns insurance commissions via CWA Insurance Services | fee-based fiduciary specializing in federal benefits |
| no commissions / no products to sell / we don't sell products | False | any insurance commission is through CWA Insurance Services, LLC and is separately disclosed |
| no proprietary products / no incentive other than your success | Implies no conflicts | we act as fiduciaries; conflicts are disclosed |
| SEC approved / government approved / endorsed by | Implies regulator endorsement | registered with [SEC/Utah]; registration does not imply skill or training |
| #1 / best / top-rated / leading advisor | Unsubstantiated superlative | (cite a specific, current, verifiable award + criteria, or drop) |

## Returns & outcomes — never say → say instead
| Never say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| guaranteed returns / guaranteed growth / we'll grow your money | designed to pursue / has the potential to / historically |
| beat the market / outperform | seeks to / aims to |
| risk-free / no risk / safe investment / can't lose | all investing involves risk, including loss of principal |
| you'll retire by X / never run out of money / retire comfortably guaranteed | may help you work toward / designed to help pursue |
| six figures over 20 years / save $X in taxes | (remove specific dollar promises; use "may help reduce" without a figure) |
| principal protection (unqualified) | (only for specific insurance products, attributed to the carrier) |

## Tax — never say → say instead
| Never say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| tax-free | tax-advantaged / potentially tax-free if requirements are met |
| eliminate your taxes / avoid taxes | may help reduce / manage the timing of taxes |
| (any tax advice as fact) | consult your qualified tax professional; CW does not provide tax advice |

## Insurance / annuity — never say → say instead
| Never say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| guaranteed income (unqualified) | guaranteed income backed by the issuing insurance company's claims-paying ability |
| safe / protected / can't lose | (state surrender charges, fees, and that guarantees rest on the carrier) |
| better than [other product]; Wall Street rips you off | (no disparagement — describe features neutrally) |

## Marketing clichés that read as misleading
unlock the secrets · secret strategy · little-known trick · loophole · what advisors won't tell you · act now or lose your benefits · once-in-a-lifetime · proven system (without proof) · always · never.

## Approved hedging vocabulary
may · can · could · designed to help · intended to · has the potential to · seeks to · historically · generally · in many cases · depending on your situation · subject to · consult your professional.
